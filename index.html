Jaynagar Store - Terms & Conditions

Jaynagar Store is an online e-commerce and local delivery platform that connects customers with registered restaurants, vendors, and delivery partners.

1. CUSTOMER TERMS & ORDER CANCELLATION

• Customers must provide correct Name, Phone Number, Delivery Address, and PIN code.
• Strict No Cancellation: Once an order has been successfully placed and confirmed, customer-initiated cancellation is NOT permitted.

2. OTP DELIVERY VERIFICATION

• Customer must share the 4-digit Delivery OTP with the delivery partner ONLY after physically receiving the order at doorstep.

3. DELIVERY CHARGES

• Orders below ₹100: Delivery Charge = ₹20.
• Orders of ₹100 or above: FREE Delivery (₹0).

4. 24x7 CUSTOMER SUPPORT
